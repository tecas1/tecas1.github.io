# Heating and making snow

Ski resorts are [making snow](https://www.theweathernetwork.com/en/news/nature/outdoors/as-ski-season-nears-a-b-c-resort-is-making-snow-in-20-degree-weather-grouse-mountain) to extend their seasons.

Could we heat their chalets at the same time?

You could put a snowmaker in the basement of the chalet. Water comes in, ice goes out, the extracted heat keeps the chalet warm.

These [L30](https://latitude90.ca/documentation/) units from [Latitude 90](https://latitude90.ca/) draw 67kW and create 60,000 kg of snow per day.

The resulting waste heat could replace 600m3 of natural gas per day saving $120 and about a tonne of emissions per day. In effect you would be heating the chalet with a highly efficient water-to-air heatpump and getting snow as a byproduct.

# High Carnot efficiency

These could be highly effective space heaters in places with abundant water and affordable electricity. 

| category | air source heat pump | snowmaker heating |
| - | - | - |
| source temperature  | -20°C ambient air | 2°C water |
| required temperature increase | 40°C | 18°C |
| efficiency | medium | high |

# Extension for cities

This idea could be extended for cities. You could use these units to heat a community centre while also making snow that could be used for skiing.
